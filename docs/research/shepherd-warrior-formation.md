# Shepherd-warrior formation: what five clocks of research say about a family "academy of war"

**In plain words:** before turning this family field lab into "Shepherd's Academy of War" (SAW), we asked one
question five times, each with a longer memory: what changed this month, this season, these two years, this
generation, and across the centuries back to the texts. This page is what survived. It feeds the five-part
article series ([series plan](../saw/SERIES.md)); it is not a build plan.

**For the engineer:** five parallel research agents, one per horizon (30 days · 30 weeks · 30 months · 30 years ·
300 years and older), each returning a dated table with every row tagged `FETCHED` (the page was opened and the
fact read there) or `UNVERIFIED`. On 2026-10-11 and 2026-10-12 the facts and quotations used in the articles were
re-fetched by script; the ones that did not reproduce were reworded or dropped. Only `FETCHED` facts
are cited below. Researched 2026-10-11.

## What each clock said

**Last 30 days (what to do tomorrow).** The word "war" beside "children" is expensive right now. A 22,111-adult
survey published 2026-09-24 ties Christian nationalism to higher support for political violence
([PRRI](https://prri.org/press-release/survey-new-report-analyzes-the-two-thirds-of-americans-who-resist-christian-nationalism/)).
In June a church's children's skit about spiritual warfare, with soldier costumes and air rifles, went viral; police
found no crime and pointed complaints to the state's child-welfare cabinet
([WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/06/30/lexington-church-faces-backlash-after-vacation-bible-school-video-goes-viral/)).
The acronym collides with a horror franchise that confirmed an eleventh film on 2026-09-24. A mainstream template
for the safe framing arrives 2026-11-20: Apple's *Way of the Warrior Kid*, whose own summary says the uncle shows
"what real courage is" instead of teaching the boy to fight
([Apple](https://www.apple.com/tv-pr/news/2026/02/apple-original-films-way-of-the-warrior-kid-starring-chris-pratt-linda-cardellini-and-jude-hill-to-premiere-globally-november-20/)).
An Olympic coach was banned for life on 2026-10-07, years after the first lawsuit.

**Last 30 weeks (the landscape).** No comparable children's program puts "war" in its name; the working words are
warrior (with a restraining code), knight, ranger, cadet, hero. Elite adults reach children through three channels:
paid bureaus (Olympians from about $15,000 an appearance,
[Key Speakers](https://keyspeakers.com/olympian-speakers)), vetted volunteers
([Travis Manion Foundation](https://www.travismanion.org/character-does-matter/): online training, a background
check and a 90-minute live session before meeting any youth; groups only; free), and recordings. Every credible
model keeps the guest in front of a group with the host adult present. Trail Life USA states it in one sentence:
a youth is never alone one-on-one with an adult who is not their parent or guardian
([FAQ](https://www.traillifeusa.com/faq/)).

**Last 30 months (the evidence).** Supported: risk the child chooses
([Canadian Paediatric Society, 2024](https://cps.ca/en/documents/position/outdoor-risky-play): remove hazards, keep
risks, wait 15 to 30 seconds); self-control as the active ingredient of martial arts
([BMC Psychology, 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11980128), correlational; one preschool trial
found a small gain in impulse control only). Contested: growth mindset, grit, universal resilience lessons (very
small average effects across 63,041 young people,
[2025 meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12171270/)). Harmful or opposed: boot-camp methods
([Campbell review, 43 studies](https://www.campbellcollaboration.org/review/effects-of-correctional-boot-camps-on-offending/));
blows to the head and chokes ([AAP, 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27940732/)); one-size mental training
for children who are already struggling ([MYRIAD, 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9340034): 8,376
students, no average benefit, worse for the high-risk group). In a 2024 survey of nearly 3,800 athletes, 89% of
those who experienced unwanted sexual behaviour never made a formal report
([SafeSport](https://uscenterforsafesport.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Culture-and-Climate-Survey-Results-Release.pdf)).

**Last 30 years (what a generation settled).** The famous claim that four mental skills raised the Navy SEAL pass
rate from 25 to 33 percent traces to a 2009 blog post about a television documentary; no primary source was found.
The Army's resilience program was launched without a pilot at a reported $125 million
([Eidelson et al., 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21967209/)) and reached more than a million soldiers
before independent review ([Steenkamp et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23597815/)). World-class adult
athletes sampled more sports, specialised later and started slower
([Güllich et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34260336/)). People tell lies from truth 54% of the time
([Bond & DePaulo, 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16859438/)), so "reading people" is not a skill to sell to
children; the intelligence profession's real method is doubting your own conclusion
([tradecraft primer](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Tradecraft-Primer-apr09.pdf)). American policing
tried the warrior frame and its reformers replaced it with "guardian"
([Rahr & Rice, 2015](https://www.ojp.gov/pdffiles1/nij/248654.pdf)). Mainstream churches put medicine and prayer
together in writing ([SBC, 2013](https://www.sbc.net/resource-library/resolutions/on-mental-health-concerns-and-the-heart-of-god/);
[Catechism 1673](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P58.HTM)). U.S. law requires that an adult and a minor
stay within "observable and interruptible distance from another adult"
([Public Law 115-126](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-115publ126/html/PLAW-115publ126.htm)).

**The long clock (300 years and back to the texts).** In Revelation 19 the armies "followed", clothed in linen,
and the only sword is in the rider's mouth (19:14–15, World English Bible). In Revelation 20:9 the saints are
surrounded and fire comes from God. Believers overcome by the Lamb's blood, their testimony and endurance (12:11;
13:10). Catholic ([Catechism 677](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P1V.HTM)), Orthodox and Protestant
readers we could open agree on who wins and disagree on the calendar. The same book gives the long war concrete
arenas: who may buy and sell (13:17), a trading empire's cargo list (18:11–13). David's fights are rescues
(1 Samuel 17:34–35); Daniel never raises a hand and renegotiates a rule with its enforcer through a ten-day test
(Daniel 1:12–13); Nehemiah's builders work with a tool in one hand and a means of defence in the other (4:17).
Plato warned that guardians must be gentle to their own or they destroy them
([Republic II](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/1497/pg1497.txt)). William James argued for a "moral
equivalent of war" aimed at hardship, not people ([1910](https://constitution.org/wj/meow.htm)). The Boys' Brigade
(1883) dropped its dummy rifles by 1926 and kept the discipline; Scouting worked once an army manual was reframed
around explorers and a promise. (Lewis's 1940 essay on chivalry and Ladd's commentary were read through secondary
pages, so the articles paraphrase them.)

## Durable state

A ★ marks a claim on which three or more clocks agree independently. A ★ claim is a candidate law. A claim from
one clock is a fact.

- ★ **No adult is ever alone with a child, and a credential is not a safeguard.** 30 years: Scouting's two-adult
  rule, the Safe Sport Act's wording. 30 months: 89% unreported. 30 weeks: Travis Manion, Trail Life and SafeSport
  policy all keep the guest in front of a group. 30 days: an Olympic coach banned years after the first complaint.
  Where the repo stands: [`docs/safety/child-safety.md`](../safety/child-safety.md) rules 5 and 6 already forbid
  private adult–child contact and unmediated matching. Nothing in the repo yet says how a guest instructor is
  vetted, because there are none.
- ★ **A fighting word is safe for children only when the enemy, the weapon and the winner are fixed in writing.**
  Long clock: Plato, Ephesians 6:12, the drift of "soldier of Christ" from allegory to crusade. 30 years: policing's
  move from warrior to guardian. 30 weeks: the one children's combat program with a published code starts with
  "Avoid the fight at all costs". 30 days: Lexington.
- ★ **The youth movements that lasted kept the discipline and dropped the props.** Long clock: Boys' Brigade,
  Scouting, Outward Bound. 30 weeks: no comparable uses "war". 30 days: Apple frames the warrior as courage.
- ★ **Wide base, slow start, small honest claims.** 30 years: Güllich, the deliberate-practice corrections, the
  Army's untested rollout. 30 months: MYRIAD, the universal-program meta-analysis. 30 weeks: every comparable's
  published evidence is self-reported satisfaction.
- **The last battle has one fighter.** One clock, but it is the primary text.
- **Medicine and prayer together.** One clock (30 years), three church bodies.

The founder's own convictions, recorded as convictions and not as findings: a defender must be trained as an
attacker (professional security agrees: a red team is "authorized … to emulate a potential adversary's attack",
[NIST](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/red_team); we found no controlled study in children); the conflict he
calls the Omega War runs through every arena (body, money, information, public life, spirit), for which the texts
above give real support; and children should be trained to break a rule in order to keep its purpose, with the
rule's guardians and not against them (Daniel 1; Chesterton's fence).

## Decision

**Write the series first. Build nothing, rename nothing, recruit no one until the series has signal.**

Rejected, each with the fact that ended it:

1. **Rebrand the repo and the live site to SAW now.** Ended by: the name's public reading is unmeasured, and the
   month's evidence says it will be read as militant by default (PRRI 2026-09-24; Lexington 2026-06-30).
2. **Keep the mission "raise warriors who can win the last battle of Revelation 19 and 20".** Ended by: the text.
   The armies' only verb is "followed" (19:14) and the saints are surrounded when fire falls from God (20:9).
3. **A Navy SEAL mental-toughness module built on the 25-to-33-percent number.** Ended by: the number traces to a
   2009 blog post retelling a documentary.
4. **Drill-and-discipline formats.** Ended by: 43 studies of boot camps, no benefit (Campbell).
5. **Striking and hard sparring for the boys.** Ended by: the AAP names blows to the head and chokes as the
   high-risk parts, and 86% of children's martial-arts concussions in one study happened in ordinary practice.
6. **Elite guests as one-to-one mentors.** Ended by: 89% of unwanted sexual behaviour unreported; the law's
   "observable and interruptible" standard; mentoring matches that end early can undo the benefit.
7. **Teaching children to "read" liars.** Ended by: 54% accuracy across 24,483 judges.
